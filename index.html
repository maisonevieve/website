Art as a Ritual.

a souvenir of a feeling

The power within us needs no tools or accessories to be accessed.

However, humans have always used rituals and spiritual items as symbols of their connection to deities, higher selves, and their bond with others. Objects that bear special meaning and purpose become reminders and symbols, and that is why at Maison Eviève, Evi creates art as a ritual. It initiates, connects deeply to the piece, and makes it a part of one's personal practice.

Evi Michailidou creates meditative art using mixed media.

Pastels, paint, thread, plaster, words, poetry. She finds inspiration in exploring the self and one's place in the world. Using gestural painting and abstract strokes, or detailed inking and embroidery, she expresses a fusion of elements often in the same piece.

12 years as a makeup artist, then further study in yoga.

Evi learned to remove herself from her art and focus on the person experiencing it. This approach is evident in her work at Maison Eviève, whether creating silk scarves, meditation letters, art prints, or a meditative art retreat.

Our creations are more than just objects.

They are reminders to pause, reflect, and connect. Through our retreats and art, we offer moments of stillness in a fast-moving world. We create art collections and gift shop items with you in mind — for those who don't need an escape from their life, just one more calming moment to keep not needing one. Welcome to Maison Eviève, where we interpret art as a souvenir of a feeling.

FAQ

A few things people usually ask.

What actually arrives in the post?

Depending on your tier, you'll receive a typed letter with a few handwritten lines and small sketches, a postcard, and — quarterly — a printed copy of "(meditating) On Art," our bilingual English/French magazine.

Does every piece of art really come with a meditation letter?

Yes — each piece is meant to be a ritual, not just an object. It arrives with its own meditation letter, so the art becomes part of your personal practice from the moment it reaches you.

Are the art pieces original or prints?

Both exist in the collection: some pieces are signed originals, others are limited prints, signed and numbered in a small edition. Either way, each is a genuine collectable — once a piece or an edition is gone, it doesn't come back.

Is the magazine really in two languages?

Yes — every issue of "(meditating) On Art" is written and printed in both English and French, side by side.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, any time, entirely on your own — no need to email me. Your welcome email includes a link to manage your subscription, where you can update your details or cancel whenever it no longer fits your life.

What makes this different from just buying art online?

Maison Eviève treats art as a souvenir of a feeling, not decoration. Every piece, letter, and issue is built around a slow, museum-aesthetic moment of stillness — a ritual you keep coming back to, not a one-time purchase.

Where are you based, and where do you ship?

Maison Eviève is based in Paris. Letters and art ship internationally — postage is calculated to keep each letter light and simple to send.
