About

Written first as letters to my father, out at sea.

Maison Eviève began the way most quiet things do — with a habit. That instinct never left. It just grew up into a place where meditation and art hold hands.

I trained as a makeup artist before I became anything else, and carried that same attention to detail into a different kind of practice.
I left Greece for Paris, and here, Maison Eviève became a place for rituals of art — a small, limited collection, a letter you can hold, a magazine built slowly, on purpose.
Everything here is meant to be a souvenir — not of a place, but of a feeling worth keeping.

FAQ

A few things people usually ask.

What actually arrives in the post?

Depending on your tier, you'll receive a typed letter with a few handwritten lines and small sketches, a postcard, and — quarterly — a printed copy of "(meditating) On Art," our bilingual English/French magazine.

Is the magazine really in two languages?

Yes — every issue of "(meditating) On Art" is written and printed in both English and French, side by side.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, any time — subscriptions are billed monthly with no long lock-in, and you're welcome to pause or stop whenever it no longer fits your life.

Are the art pieces original or prints?

Pieces from the Mesdames collection are signed and numbered prints of the original ink and pastel work, kept to a small edition size.

Where are you based, and where do you ship?

Maison Eviève is based in Paris. Letters and art ship internationally — postage is calculated to keep each letter light and simple to send.
