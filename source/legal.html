Data Protection

What we collect

  • Name, shipping address, and email address, when you place an order or subscribe (collected and processed via Stripe)
  • Email address, when you sign up for the newsletter or letter subscription (collected and processed via MailerLite)
  • Correspondence you send us directly by email

Why we collect it

  • To fulfil orders and subscriptions — printing and posting letters, magazines, and art (legal basis: performance of a contract)
  • To send subscription communications (legal basis: performance of a contract) and, if you've opted in, marketing emails (legal basis: consent)
  • To comply with French accounting and tax record-keeping obligations (legal basis: legal obligation)

Who we share it with

We use trusted third-party processors to run the site and business: Stripe (payment processing), MailerLite (email delivery), and Cloudflare (hosting). Each operates under its own privacy policy and data protection safeguards. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

How long we keep it

Order and invoicing data is retained for 10 years, in accordance with French accounting obligations (Article L123-22 of the French Commercial Code), and for up to 6 years for tax purposes. Marketing email data is kept until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these rights, email bonjour@maisonevieve.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL.

Refunds & Cancellations

At Maison Eviève, every product — including original artworks, not only prints, letters, and magazines — is made or finished to order, rather than pulled from ready-made stock. Originals are completed only once an order is placed: final brushwork, finishing touches, or a personal dedication are carried out specifically for that order.

Under Article L221-28 of the French Consumer Code, the standard 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply to goods that are custom-made or clearly personalized to a consumer's specifications. Because every piece sold here — original or reproduction — is completed after and because of your order, this legal exception applies, and all sales are final once production has begun.

Order Cancellations

You may cancel your order within 24 hours of purchase, provided production has not yet started. Email bonjour@maisonevieve.com as soon as possible to check whether cancellation is still possible.

Final Sale Policy

Because every product is custom-made to order, we do not accept returns or exchanges for change of mind. Please review product descriptions, sizes, and dimensions carefully before purchasing.

This final-sale policy does not affect the statutory guarantee of conformity (Articles L217-3 et seq. of the French Consumer Code) or the guarantee against hidden defects (Articles 1641 et seq. of the French Civil Code), which apply in all cases, regardless of any commercial policy on our part.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, or you receive the wrong item, we will provide a free replacement. Email bonjour@maisonevieve.com within 7 days of receiving your order, including:

  • Your order number

We will arrange a replacement as soon as possible once this is confirmed.

Lost Packages

If your package is lost in transit, we will work with the shipping carrier to locate it. If it cannot be recovered, we will offer either store credit or a replacement. Contact us if you suspect your order has been lost.
