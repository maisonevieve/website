The First Letter

"Before a letter arrives in your mailbox, there is a moment of anticipation. This is that moment, imagined."

Welcome, dear one, and thank you for joining. Find below the letter experience I promised you in audio and text form. Below that, you'll find a sample issue of "(meditating)) On Art" magazine. See you there!

The Letter Experience

Dear one,

Receiving and sending letters has today a romantic and nostalgic feel, long from the necessity and uncertainty of the past. In this digital letter we will see what it feels like to receive a letter. Experiencing it in your mind before it comes true. Before we begin a sensory meditation, please sit in place where you can ignore distractions. Take three deep breaths, filling your belly fully, and exhaling completely. Relax your face, relax your neck.

I want you to bring to mind a voice you find very relaxing. It could be by someone you know, or even a celebrity, just make it a voice, pace, and even accent, that is relaxing, possibly a little whimsical. Try to read the rest of the letter in this voice.

Imagine receiving a letter. It surprised you in your mailbox, maybe among bills or advertising flyers. It's in a colorful envelope and has your name on it. You take it inside, curious, you leave all the rest on the table, and focus on this mysterious letter. It fills a little heavier, it must contain more than one page. You open it slowly.

As if the scene is unfolding in slow motion, listen to the sound of tearing the envelop, feel the texture of the paper between your fingers, smell even, the paper and what it contains.

You unfold the paper and start reading it. As you do, you imagine the sound of a keyboard typing the words you read. And the fountain pen scribbling the handwritten part on the back of the page. Breathe slowly, like you're savoring the letter, the breath...

You finish reading the letter and then focus on the postcard that came with it. There is a piece of art printed on it, and a meditation prompt on the back. You'll decide later whether you'll keep it, or send it to someone. It's been a while since you've mailed a physical postcard...

You keep the letter and the postcard in your lap, filling their barely-there weight, and you sit in silence. You think about what you read. How it has affected you. Feel any sensations in your body... I imagine you sitting in a burgundy armchair, the afternoon sun, bathing your space, and some dried flowers on your windowsill seem to be smiling. Imagine yourself like this too. Feel the warmth of the sun on your skin. Feel the velvety fabric of the armchair supporting your head. Imagine your real life being a dream you're having on an afternoon nap in that chair...

And then, breathe...

Now let this letter go and replay the experience in your mind, on your own. Sitting in silence, after any practice, is vital. If you like, make this experience true by subscribing for a monthly Art Meditation Letter. Either way, I've included a printable stationary set, with a postcard and an envelop. Maybe you could recreate this experience for someone who would care.

Take as much time as you need with this letter, and meet me at the next one.

Love,
Evi
